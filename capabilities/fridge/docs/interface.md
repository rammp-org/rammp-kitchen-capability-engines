# Fridge Capability — Interface

**Rung:** ad-hoc (declared by this module, not standardized)
**Owner:** @dhyi-svg
**Tier:** experimental

## What it does

Opens and closes the fridge door with the Kinova arm. On an `open` goal it finds the handle with SAM 3
on the wrist camera (`detect_camera`, `handle_source`) and measures the door's direction by fitting
the printed handle's model (`RAMMP.Fridge.Handle5.STL`) to the handle's depth (`door_normal: handle_model`, the side grasp's method); two looks must agree within 3°. It grasps the handle
squared to the door (at the middle of the bar, or `grasp_offset_m` above or below it), the gripper
closing as the pull starts, pulls the door 60° open and runs on 8° further while the gripper opens,
then, in one motion, backs off the handle, goes round the door's free edge and pushes the door on to
90° with the side of the hand, steps 10 cm off it and, in the same motion, turns to face into the fridge in front
of its middle (if the arm can reach that). If the push needs the wheelchair moved first, the goal stops after the
back-off and its result (`note`) and feedback say how far; an `open` goal with `chair_moved` then
pushes. With `push_open: false` it pulls 90° instead, lets go the same way and goes round the door's
free edge to its inner side, facing the fridge, always ending upright. A `close` goal pushes the door
to about 45° with the hand (which may hold a container, never tilted) and it swings shut by itself.
Everything is planned before anything moves; a failed plan or check stops the run with the arm
holding where it is. Without it, no kitchen task can reach anything in the fridge.

**The arm moves only if the node runs with `allow_execute: true` and the goal sets
`execute`.** Otherwise every goal is a dry run: detect, plan and gate, nothing commanded.

**The base moves only if, in addition, the goal sets `drive_base`.** Both actions take
`drive_base` (bool, default false) and `base_speed` (m/s, default 0.05, at most `base_max_speed`). With it, an
executing goal drives the wheelchair moves the plan needs itself (`self.base`: TwistSetpoint
velocities, MIB in AUTONOMOUS on the `low` profile), closed loop on `/base/odom`, a fixed distance
of at most `base_max_distance` straight along the chair's heading, ending within `base_tolerance`, and goes on in
the same goal: no `chair_moved` goal (sending both is refused). A move that stops part way (wrong way, further than
asked, timeout, arm e-stop, joystick touch, stale odometry) fails the goal with error code `base_stopped`, the base
commanded still and the arm holding. Without it the chair is moved by hand, as below.

**Planning: cuRobo plans every motion.** Each motion is a few cuRobo legs to tool poses
(`planning.py`), each against its own collision world: the cabinet, the door at its angle and the
handle (`world.py`), written to `planner_world_dir` and loaded into the planner with `set_world`,
plus the planner's own wheelchair layers and the scene camera. A leg onto or along the door leaves
out what the hand touches (the handle and the door round the hand) and is solved along its exact line
or arc by local IK. The push-open's push is checked step by step against the door where it then is:
its front 2.5 cm and edges always, its inner part except round the hand. cuRobo picks the arm's configuration for every pose, with its joint
limits; if no grasp's swing plans from the configuration it picks, the line-up is planned again from
other configurations (elbow turned, wrist flipped). cuRobo's blend joins each motion's legs into one
trajectory, cutting the corners between them, which the driver runs. The swing and everything after
the release run under the driver's stiffest joint impedance. Each search (the whole plan before
anything moves, and each re-plan after the arm or the base has moved) has `PLAN_BUDGET_S` (120 s);
past it, it refuses. The time spent detecting, moving or driving never counts against it. If the swing
stops short, the gripper is not opened afterwards. The robot's own planner world is put back when the
action ends.

The open saves the door's geometry (`<data_dir>/door.json`) for the close; every run is logged
to `<data_dir>/runs/<time>_open/` (events, every trajectory sent, arm state at 25 Hz, camera
frames, everything the node logged during it in `console.log`, and a rosbag in `bag/` unless
`record_bag` is off) and every detection frame to `<data_dir>/detections/`.

| Action  | Params                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Result payload                                     |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `open`  | `execute` (bool, default false), `speed` ((0, 1\], default 1.0: scales every arm motion), `swing_deg` (30–110, at most 85 with the push; default 60, or 90 with `push_open` false: exactly this swing or it refuses), `push_open` (bool, default true), `grasp_offset_m` (m, default 0: grasp this far above the middle of the handle bar, below if negative; refused beyond ±(`handle_length_m`/2 − 3 cm)), `release_only` (bool, default false: let go and back off the handle, after a stopped run), `chair_moved` (bool, default false: after the push-open asked for a wheelchair move and it was made, only the push), `save_door` (bool, default false: a dry run that plans OK also writes the door file as the planned open would leave it, for testing the close) | `{"executed": bool, "note": str, "runs_dir": str}` |
| `close` | `execute` as above, `speed` (default 0.7: at least that, or the swing doesn't carry the door shut), plus the settings below                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `{"executed": bool, "note": str, "runs_dir": str}` |

`close` needs the door file the open saved (an executed open, or a dry run with `save_door`). The gripper is never touched (it may hold a container)
and the hand never tilts: every planned configuration keeps its roll and pitch within 2° of where it
starts, or the close refuses before moving.

| Setting                           | Default         | Meaning                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `chair_back`                      | unset           | Wheelchair move first (m, + back, - forward); unset: the smallest that plans, up to 35 cm. `0`: none   |
| `chair_moved`                     | `false`         | The wheelchair has been moved as the plan said. Required to execute with a move; a dry run plans as if |
| `close_to_deg`                    | `45.0`          | Swing the door shut until it is open this far (deg); it swings shut by itself from there               |
| `door_deg`                        | door file       | How far the door is open now (deg). Give it if the door was moved by hand                              |
| `push_radius` / `push_radius_end` | `0.30` / `0.28` | The hand's distance from the hinge during the swing, start / end (m)                                   |
| `push_z`                          | searched        | Height of the swing (m); refused outside the door. Default: the first height within it that passes     |
| `hold_offset_x`                   | `-0.1651`       | Plan with the door shifted in x (m): the 6.5 in container. `0` if not holding it                       |
| `side_past_hinge`                 | `0.08`          | Swing start, sideways past the hinge (m)                                                               |
| `retreat_dist`                    | `0.20`          | Back straight out up to this far after the swing (m)                                                   |

Failures: `refused` (a check failed; the message says which and the arm holds), `stopped`
(a motion stopped short), `bad_params`. On an executed success it marks the object `opened`
(true after `open`, false after `close`). Nothing measures whether the door shut: watch it.

Registers with `/rammp_orchestrator/register_engine` on startup, retrying until
the orchestrator is up.

## Before a run

- **Start pose.** Nothing moves the camera to the fridge. The arm starts pointed at the closed door,
  roughly square-on, 50–60 cm from it, with the handle in view and the door filling most of the
  image.
- **Placement.** The open prints where the wheelchair should go and refuses with a suggestion when
  it does not fit. At the placement that passed (handle x 0.50–0.56, y −0.45 to −0.39), a 90° door's
  free edge is ~10 cm in front of the arm's base, so the hand passes right beside the base (~0.67 m
  up) and sweeps across in front of it. The sim has no wheelchair or person in it: keep that space
  clear.
- **Measure** a new fridge: the `*_m` parameters below. A wrong hinge drags the door or the fridge
  during the swing.
- **Watch where the fingers close:** `GRIP_EXT` is the microwave's and nothing checks the grasp on
  this handle. If the fingers hit the door or close short of the bar, change it (bigger = shorter).

How to run it on the arm: [TESTING.md](TESTING.md).

## Known limits

- **The wheelchair is the planner's own model.** On the 30° mount the wheelchair, seat and occupant
  are the cuRobo planner's collision layers for flatbot, in every world it plans in; the fridge's
  pushed world only adds the fridge and the scene camera. Their measurements are best guesses apart
  from the arm's mount, and cuRobo plans nothing from a start pose inside them: the 2026-11-04 runs'
  start sits 3–10 cm inside the armrest and torso, 2026-11-06's 8–17 mm inside the armrest. The open
  refuses at once, naming the boxes; start from a pose outside them (the web app's Fridge pose is).
  The arm's own collision spheres (flatbot's `curobo/robot.yaml`) decide how close it may come: the
  file's 47 spheres reach 16–65 mm past the arm's meshes and miss parts of them (up to 2.5 cm at the
  finger tips); a 197-sphere fit to the meshes (≤ 12 mm past them) gets within about a centimetre of the
  old PyBullet planner's reach next to the occupant's lap.
- **The scene camera is a fixed box** (`SCENE_CAMERA_XYZ`, flatbot's on 2026-11-06, from TF), not read
  from TF: move the camera and it is wrong.
- **The door's direction** (`door_normal: handle_model`) was measured on 2026-11-06 scene camera frames (±1° per look,
  handle 0.7–0.9 m away), not yet on the wrist camera's. The grasp corrections (`GRIP_EXT`, ...) were set with the
  wrist camera's handle point.
- **The 30° mount is sim-checked only.** Set `mount_tilt_deg: 30`. The planning runs in `arm_mount`,
  which is level on every robot; the parameter sets the reach limits and placement advice. All
  hardware runs were on a level arm. On the 30° model the 90° open and the close plan with the
  fridge ~14–22 cm further from the arm than on the level one. The 60° pull + push-open plans
  with the handle at its 2026-11-01 height (~0.31 m above the arm base) because the 30° mount
  pushes from 10 cm above the handle and lets the grasp tilt up to 45° (`MOUNTS[30]`). With the
  level arm's values (10 cm below, up to 25°) it plans almost nowhere at that height.
- **The wheelchair is moved by a person** unless the goal sets `drive_base`. An action can't
  prompt, so `chair_moved` stands in for the old script's prompt. `drive_base` uses `self.base`
  (the engine's drive base interface), so `required_resources` lists `base`. It has not yet run on
  the base.
- **`handle_source: sam3` is unmeasured here.** Its grasp offsets were never measured on this
  fridge: re-measure before using it. Depth stays the code's default; TESTING.md runs SAM 3, which
  found the handle where depth didn't on 2026-11-04.
- **A glossy door's depth can be its reflection.** On 2026-11-04 the black door read 0.9–1.1 m
  everywhere behind a handle at 0.5 m. The handle then comes from its own depth, but no door plane
  fits it and the open refuses ("no door plane fits the handle"). A matte surface on the door
  around the handle fixes it.
- **The push-open does not keep the door open.** Its one hardware run (2026-10-24) finished its
  trajectory and the door swung shut.
- **The close has hit the handle.** On 2026-10-24 the hand shut the door by hitting the handle, not
  the door face, and carried on with the planned swing after the door had shut.
- **The close has hit the wheelchair.** Before any keep-out, a close ran the arm into the back of
  the chair (2026-10-24). It has not been run on the arm since.
- **The container is not in the collision model.** The plan only keeps the hand back by its size.

Per-rig constants that are not parameters yet, in `fridge/constants.py`: the grasp corrections
(`GRIP_EXT`, `GRASP_X_ADJUST`, `VERTICAL_CORR`), the handle gates and the routes round the door. Per mount
(`MOUNTS`): the reach limits, the placement window, the push-open's hand height (`PUSH_OPEN_DZ_M`) and the grasp's
nose-down tilts (`GRASP_PITCHES_DEG`, 15–45° only: under 15° the fingers don't grip the bar firmly enough): on the
level arm 15° and 25°, on the 30° mount 15°, 30° and 45°.

`self.arm` reads these `arm.*` parameters once at startup and refuses changes
afterwards. They are set per robot like any other; `arm.mock_urdf` names the
URDF `make_sim()` loads in mock runs (the real driver supplies its own).
`arm.sender_id` is the one left out of the real fragment: its default is the
engine id, so each capability sends its goals under its own name.

## Hardware

No devices of its own: it reaches the arm, the gripper and the wrist camera through their
drivers' topics. It needs the arm with a 2F-85 gripper, a wrist RGB-D camera with depth aligned
to colour, and a calibrated camera tf (hand-eye) into `arm.frame`. It needs no GPU.
